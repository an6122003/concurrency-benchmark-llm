Lab 1: Aurora says it runs on the RTX Spark

The fine-tuning examples came from the DGX Spark edition, so the tuned model
told an RTX Spark audience "I run entirely on this NVIDIA DGX Spark". The
dataset now names the RTX Spark (28 examples; regenerated with
data/build_dataset.py, nothing else changes), and so does the lab's README.

setup.ps1 records which training examples the model was trained on and
trains it again when an update brings different ones, so laptops that are
already installed get the corrected Aurora on their next update (about a
minute and a half on the GPU).
